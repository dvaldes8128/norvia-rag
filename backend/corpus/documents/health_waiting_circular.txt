NORVIA HEALTH CIRCULAR HC-55 — WAITING PERIODS

Elective orthopaedic surgery carries a waiting period of one hundred eighty days
from the effective date of the health plan. Maternity benefits become available
after two hundred seventy days of continuous membership. Emergency trauma care
has no waiting period and is covered from day one. Preexisting cardiac conditions
declared at underwriting remain subject to a twelve month moratorium. Dental
implants are excluded during the first year and thereafter reimbursed at sixty
percent of the fee schedule. Members transferring from another Norvia group plan
may carry over waiting credit for elective orthopaedic surgery when there is no
coverage gap longer than thirty days.
