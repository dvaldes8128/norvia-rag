NORVIA HOME POLICY — FLOOD ENDORSEMENT HF-2024

This endorsement applies to residential dwellings insured under the Norvia Home
policy series. Flood damage is covered up to fifty thousand EUR under section A
when water originates from overflowing rivers, storm surge, or sudden surface
runoff. The insured must report flood incidents within seventy two hours of
discovery. A flood deductible of one thousand five hundred EUR applies per
occurrence. Gradual seepage through foundations is excluded from flood cover.
Sandbagging and emergency pumping costs are reimbursable up to five hundred EUR
when arranged by an approved Norvia contractor. This flood endorsement does not
cover vehicles parked in underground garages.
