NORVIA MOTOR POLICY — THEFT COVER MT-118

Comprehensive motor cover includes theft of the insured vehicle and forced entry
damage to locked compartments. Vehicle theft indemnification requires proof that
both factory keys were surrendered and that an immobiliser was active at the time
of loss. The maximum payout for vehicle theft equals the market value listed in
the schedule minus a five percent theft excess. Personal belongings left inside
the cabin are covered only up to three hundred EUR. Joyriding by household
members is excluded from theft benefits. The policyholder must notify the police
and Norvia claims within twenty four hours of discovering a vehicle theft.
