NORVIA CLAIMS PROCEDURE NOTICE CP-3

To open a claim the policyholder submits the online notice form or calls the
twenty four hour claims line. Required documents include the policy schedule,
incident description, and any police report reference number. Photographic
evidence should be uploaded within five business days. An assigned adjuster
contacts the insured within forty eight hours of a complete notice. Settlement
offers for property damage under ten thousand EUR may be issued without a site
visit when remote assessment is conclusive. Disputed settlements escalate to the
internal review panel within fifteen days of written objection. Fraudulent
statements void indemnity for the affected claim event.
