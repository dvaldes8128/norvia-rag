NORVIA LIABILITY RIDER — WAR AND SANCTIONS EXCLUSION LX-9

Public liability cover excludes injury or property damage arising directly or
indirectly from war, invasion, hostilities, civil war, rebellion, or military
usurpation. Cyber operations attributed to a nation state are treated as warlike
acts under this exclusion. Nuclear contamination and biological weapon events are
likewise excluded without exception. Sanctions compliance: Norvia will not pay
any claim that would expose the insurer to penalties under United Nations or
European Union sanctions lists. Routine industrial accidents unrelated to
hostilities remain covered under the standard liability schedule. Brokers must
disclose known exposure to conflict zones at binding.
